Refactor: unify chat prefixes and fix economy feature toggle bugs

- Replaced hardcoded "[ServerManagement]" and "[SM+]" strings with `Constants.CHAT_PREFIX` across commands, client handlers, GUI packets, and update hooks in both Forge and Fabric.

- Fixed `SaveEconomySettingsPacket` in Forge to force-clear caches (`clearCache()`) for economy configs so subsequent reads fetch the updated values.
