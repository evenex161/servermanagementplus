<h1>Server Management Plus</h1>

<p><strong>The all-in-one server management solution for Minecraft</strong></p>

<p><strong>Supported</strong>: Minecraft 1.20.1 (Forge 47.4.0+) | Minecraft 1.21.1 (Forge 52.1.0+ / NeoForge 21.1.222+)</p>

<p>Server Management Plus gives you a complete suite of tools to run your server — a full economy with bank accounts, a player marketplace, a casino, daily tasks, world management, and a sleek admin dashboard — all in one mod.</p>

<hr>

<h2>Features</h2>

<h3>Admin Dashboard</h3>
<p>A modern dark-themed command center accessible with <code>/sm</code>. Six feature cards let you jump straight into World Manager, Player Manager, Console, Global Settings, Economy Management, or Mod Settings. Every screen has consistent navigation with back/close buttons and a polished header bar.</p>

<p><strong>Live Server Console</strong> — Run server commands directly from the GUI. Output is color-coded by log level (red for errors, yellow for warnings) and scrollable. Requires OP level 2.</p>

<p><strong>Global Settings</strong> — Toggle Chat Isolation (separate chat per dimension) and Tab Isolation (separate tab list per dimension) with iOS-style toggle switches.</p>

<p><strong>Mod Settings</strong> — Enable or disable individual features (World Manager, Player Manager, SlimeHead, Economy) without restarting the server.</p>

<hr>

<h3>Economy System</h3>
<p>Every player gets a bank account on first join with a configurable starting balance (default: $1,000).</p>

<ul>
<li><strong>Balance management</strong> — Check your balance, view transaction history, and track every dollar</li>
<li><strong>Player-to-player transfers</strong> — Send money directly or create money requests that the other player can accept or deny</li>
<li><strong>Transaction history</strong> — Full audit trail of all purchases, sales, transfers, task rewards, gambling, and free claims with type-specific icons</li>
<li><strong>Action bar notifications</strong> — Non-intrusive success/error messages displayed above the hotbar</li>
<li><strong>Admin tools</strong> — Set, give, or take money from any player via commands or the admin dashboard</li>
<li><strong>Economy Statistics</strong> — Real-time economy overview: total accounts, money in circulation, inflation rate, marketplace activity, gambling stats, and transaction volumes</li>
<li><strong>Encrypted storage</strong> — All economy data secured with AES-256-GCM encryption</li>
</ul>

<hr>

<h3>MineBay Marketplace</h3>
<p>A fully-featured player marketplace where you can buy and sell items with other players.</p>

<ul>
<li><strong>Create listings</strong> — Sell any item or stack from your inventory</li>
<li><strong>Flexible pricing</strong> — Accept money, up to 3 different item types, or both</li>
<li><strong>Dynamic market pricing</strong> — Supply/demand tracking with configurable inflation</li>
<li><strong>Counteroffers</strong> — Buyers can propose alternative prices; sellers review and accept/deny</li>
<li><strong>Overflow inventory</strong> — If your inventory is full when buying, items are safely stored for later pickup (works in both survival and creative mode)</li>
<li><strong>Item safety</strong> — Server-authoritative item handling with escrow rollback. Items are never lost or duplicated</li>
<li><strong>Draft system</strong> — Step-by-step listing creation with item picker and quantity controls</li>
<li><strong>Margin controls</strong> — Sellers can adjust prices from -50% to +200% of market value</li>
</ul>

<hr>

<h3>MineStacks Casino</h3>
<p>A full casino experience with four games, all using <code>SecureRandom</code> for provably fair results.</p>

<ul>
<li><strong>Coin Flip</strong> — 2% house edge, 2x payout. Simple heads or tails</li>
<li><strong>Dice Roll</strong> — 3% house edge. Bet on High/Low (2x), Seven (5x), or Doubles (6x)</li>
<li><strong>Slot Machine</strong> — 5% house edge. Payouts from Cherry (2x) up to Jackpot (100x), with half payouts for two-of-a-kind</li>
<li><strong>Roulette</strong> — 2.7% house edge. Bet on Red/Black, Even/Odd, Low/High for 2x returns</li>
</ul>

<p>Supports both money bets ($10–$10,000) and item bets (auto-valued). Includes spinning animations, particle effects on wins, screen shake, and sound effects. Your gambling stats are tracked.</p>

<hr>

<h3>World Management</h3>
<p>Manage all server worlds from a visual interface.</p>

<ul>
<li><strong>World list</strong> — See all dimensions with player counts</li>
<li><strong>Teleportation</strong> — Click to teleport between worlds (with cooldown)</li>
<li><strong>Portal control</strong> — Toggle Nether and End portals on or off</li>
<li><strong>Lobby spawn</strong> — Set a lobby spawn point and teleport players to it</li>
<li><strong>Chat isolation</strong> — Keep chat messages per-dimension</li>
<li><strong>Tab isolation</strong> — Separate the tab list by dimension</li>
<li><strong>Timer system</strong> — Auto-enable/disable portals on a schedule</li>
</ul>

<hr>

<h3>Player Manager</h3>
<p>Admin tools for managing online players.</p>

<ul>
<li><strong>Player list</strong> — View all online players with location, dimension, and gamemode</li>
<li><strong>Spectate</strong> — Watch any player in real-time</li>
<li><strong>View inventory</strong> — Inspect a player's inventory</li>
<li><strong>Kick/Ban</strong> — Remove or ban players directly from the GUI</li>
</ul>

<hr>

<h3>SlimeHead</h3>
<p>A fun cosmetic item — an unbreakable slime head block with a custom texture.</p>

<ul>
<li>Obtained via <code>/slimehead</code> command or a 5% drop chance from slimes</li>
<li>Protected from breaking by non-ops when placed</li>
<li>Available in the Creative Tools &amp; Utilities tab</li>
</ul>

<hr>

<h3>MOTD Editor</h3>
<p>Customize your server's Message of the Day with a visual editor.</p>

<ul>
<li><strong>Rich text formatting</strong> — Apply color codes and formatting (bold, italic, underline, strikethrough) with a live preview</li>
<li><strong>Multi-line support</strong> — Edit both lines of the server MOTD</li>
<li><strong>Admin-only</strong> — Accessible from the Dashboard, requires OP level 2</li>
<li><strong>Instant apply</strong> — Changes take effect immediately without server restart</li>
<li><strong>Toggleable</strong> — Enable/disable the custom MOTD via config</li>
</ul>

<hr>

<h3>Notification System</h3>
<p>Clickable chat notifications keep players informed without being spammy.</p>

<ul>
<li><strong>Login notifications</strong> — Admins see a dashboard link; players see free reward and task reminders</li>
<li><strong>Payment alerts</strong> — Notifications when you send or receive money</li>
<li><strong>Task completion</strong> — Instant notification with a clickable claim button when a task is done</li>
<li><strong>Reward claimed</strong> — Confirmation when rewards are collected</li>
</ul>

<hr>

<h3>Help Integration</h3>
<p>All Server Management commands are integrated into the vanilla <code>/help</code> command.</p>

<ul>
<li><code>/help</code> — Lists all available commands including Server Management ones with descriptions</li>
<li><code>/help &lt;command&gt;</code> — Detailed usage for any mod command (e.g., <code>/help bank</code>)</li>
<li><strong>Admin filtering</strong> — Admin commands tagged with [Admin] and hidden from non-ops</li>
<li>Works alongside vanilla and other mods' commands</li>
</ul>

<hr>

<h3>OTA Update System</h3>
<p>Automatic over-the-air mod updates for connected clients.</p>

<ul>
<li>Detects client/server version mismatch automatically</li>
<li>Downloads the correct JAR, verifies hash integrity, and installs</li>
<li>Shows an update progress screen to the player</li>
<li>Smart version comparison: semantic versioning first, then build number for same-version patches</li>
<li><strong>Multi-version aware</strong> — OTA updates are blocked across different Minecraft versions (e.g., a 1.20.1 client won't receive a 1.21.1 update)</li>
<li><strong>Build number tracking</strong> — Each release uses <code>v2.0.0-bXX</code> format to differentiate incremental builds within the same version</li>
</ul>

<hr>

<h3>Security</h3>
<p>All sensitive data is encrypted at rest and authenticated in transit.</p>

<ul>
<li><strong>AES-256-GCM encryption</strong> for economy data, daily tasks, money requests, and achievement tracking</li>
<li><strong>Unique server key</strong> auto-generated on first run, stored with restricted permissions</li>
<li><strong>HMAC-SHA256</strong> packet authentication prevents replay and tampering</li>
<li><strong>Session management</strong> with per-player tokens and 30-minute timeout</li>
<li><strong>Atomic transactions</strong> with automatic escrow rollback — no partial operations, no data loss</li>
<li><strong>Item duplication prevention</strong> — Server-authoritative item validation in marketplace listings and offers; race-condition-safe slot clearing; synchronized balance operations</li>
<li><strong>Network buffer hardening</strong> — All 54 packet string fields enforce strict length limits (<code>readUtf(N)</code>) to prevent memory exhaustion from oversized payloads</li>
<li><strong>NBT size limits</strong> — All NBT data loading uses 10MB NbtAccounter limits to prevent memory exhaustion from corrupted files</li>
<li><strong>Input validation</strong> — Player names validated against <code>[a-zA-Z0-9_]{1,16}</code> regex at the network layer before any server-side processing</li>
<li><strong>Log injection prevention</strong> — User-controlled strings are sanitized before logging to prevent log forging</li>
<li><strong>Thread-safe economy</strong> — Synchronized balance operations, atomic escrow with try-catch rollback, and snapshot-based collection iteration prevent concurrency exploits</li>
</ul>

<h2>Installation</h2>

<h3>Requirements</h3>
<ul>
<li><strong>Minecraft</strong> 1.20.1 or 1.21.1</li>
<li><strong>Forge</strong> 47.4.0+ (MC 1.20.1) or 52.1.0+ (MC 1.21.1), <strong>or NeoForge</strong> 21.1.222+ (MC 1.21.1)</li>
<li><strong>Java</strong> 17+ (MC 1.20.1) or 21+ (MC 1.21.1)</li>
</ul>

<h3>Setup</h3>
<ol>
<li>Download the JAR for your Minecraft version and mod loader:
<ul>
<li>MC 1.20.1 Forge: <code>servermanagementplus-v2.0.0-b01-mc1.20.1-forge-release.jar</code></li>
<li>MC 1.21.1 Forge: <code>servermanagementplus-v2.0.0-b01-mc1.21.1-forge-release.jar</code></li>
<li>MC 1.21.1 NeoForge: <code>servermanagementplus-v2.0.0-b01-mc1.21.1-neoforge-release.jar</code></li>
</ul>
</li>
<li>Place it in your server's <code>mods/</code> folder</li>
<li>Start the server — config and data folders generate automatically</li>
<li>Optionally install on clients for full GUI support (server-side only works too)</li>
<li>Adjust settings in <code>config/servermanagement-common.toml</code> and restart</li>
</ol>

<h3>First-Time Setup (Server Owners)</h3>
<ol>
<li>Review config values (starting balance, rewards, cooldowns)</li>
<li>Open the admin dashboard with <code>/sm</code> to explore features</li>
<li>Go to <strong>Economy Management</strong> to create daily task templates</li>
<li>Set free reward amounts and items</li>
<li>Test the economy flow with <code>/bank</code>, <code>/minebay</code>, and <code>/minestacks</code></li>
</ol>

<h2>Troubleshooting</h2>

<table>
<thead>
<tr><th>Problem</th><th>Solution</th></tr>
</thead>

<tr><td>Transaction failed</td><td>Check chat error message — transactions auto-rollback, no data is lost</td></tr>
</tbody>
</table>
